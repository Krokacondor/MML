# Mario Mania League

The stat-tracking website for the Mario Mania League (MML), a CPU vs CPU Mario Super Sluggers league.

Site: https://krokacondor.github.io/MML/

## What is on it

- **Home**: league leaders, standings, latest scores, and stories from the Press Box
- **Teams**: every team's logo, roster, totals, general manager, and game log
- **Players**: a card for every player, with season-by-season stats, career totals, awards, and the teams they have played for
- **Stats**: every stat in sortable tables, for players or teams
- **Scores**: every game, with box scores (scores stay hidden until you choose to see them)
- **Awards**: season awards, the All-Stars, and the All-Star Game
- **League Banter**: posts from around the league

The season menu at the top of the page switches between seasons.

This is a fan project and is not affiliated with Nintendo.
